# Descriptive writing profile

Classify observable properties before considering any reporting scale. The classifier
does not choose a score and should not guess what an expert assigned.

For topic development, separately observe task engagement, position stability,
support breadth, specificity, linkage between support and position, explanation depth,
organization, idea advancement rather than repetition, functional completeness, and
internal consistency. Formal paragraph breaks are not required for functional
organization. A counterargument is evidence only when present; its absence is not a
defect by itself.

For conventions, separately observe intelligibility interruption, the scope of repair
needed, grammar, sentence-boundary and syntax control, usage, punctuation, orthography,
the dispersion of credible errors, syntactic ambition, and consistency of control.
Ambition describes the structures attempted; it is not automatically evidence of
quality. Verify automated flags in the response before describing their impact.

Do not collapse the dimensions into one impression. The downstream compositor, not
the classifier, decides how descriptions interact.

# Topic-development teacher distinctions

Ask what changed for the reader after each unit of support. Naming supplies a point;
illustrating supplies a case; explaining supplies a cause, consequence, comparison,
or significance. These operations can occur in simple language.

Check the response as a whole. A single impressive sentence cannot supply missing
development elsewhere, and a rough sentence cannot erase otherwise visible reasoning.
Require observable evidence for both the proposed description and its nearest
stronger alternative.

# Deterministic evidence for conventions

Use local grammar, spelling, sentence-shape, and dispersion measurements as an
auditable index into the response. Verify important flags in context because names,
dialect, code-switching, and deliberate style can produce false positives. Collapse
copies of the same spelling problem conceptually rather than counting each as new.

Give special attention to credible boundary, syntax, and grammar patterns distributed
across sentences. Treat counts as answers to how often and how widely, never as a
score or expert-label proxy.

# Topic-development analytic profile

Describe these dimensions independently: relation to the task; clarity and stability
of the position; number of distinct supports; how much support is unpacked; consistency
of specificity; explicit links from evidence to position; functional organization;
and advancement across the response.

Do not average dimensions into a halo. Look for bottlenecks: relevant material can
remain limited when its importance is left implicit, while a modest number of supports
can be effective when each performs clear reasoning work.

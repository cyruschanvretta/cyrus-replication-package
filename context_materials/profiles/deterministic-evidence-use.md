# Using deterministic response evidence

Use deterministic evidence to make the reading traceable, especially for Conventions.
First apply hard authored rules: blank/non-linguistic status and the explicit CV
under-30-word rule. Then inspect the location, recurrence, and category spread of flags.

LanguageTool messages and built-in possible-fragment/run-on signals may be false
positives. Verify each important pattern against the response. Collapse repeated
instances of the same spelling problem conceptually. Give greater weight to sentence
structure or grammar patterns that force rereading than to isolated typography.

Counts help answer “how often?” and “how widely distributed?”, but the band still turns
on interference, distraction, non-distraction, or demonstrated control. For Topic
Development and Open Response, do not treat convention signals as quality evidence.

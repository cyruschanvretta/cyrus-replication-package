# Topic-development annotation patterns

Rater descriptions repeatedly distinguish naming from unpacking. Several generic
reasons provide breadth but not necessarily depth. One concrete detail can be specific
but still poorly linked. Treat breadth, specificity, linkage, and explanation as
independent witnesses.

Mechanical transitions and conventional paragraphs do not by themselves establish
progression. Functional organization may be visible through a stable position,
purposeful grouping, and a sequence in which later ideas add consequences or
significance. Repetition may preserve focus while failing to advance it.

# Using linguistic feature packs

Feature packs describe observable properties of the current response. They do
not contain question-specific examples, expert labels, response-set membership,
or score thresholds. A measurement can direct attention to a passage, but the
classification must still be justified by the response.

For TD, use the structure pack only to decide where to inspect development.
Length, lexical variety, sentence variety, readability, and clean conventions
do not prove that a reason has been explained or connected to the position.

For CV, combine reader impact with several views of control: flag type,
frequency, sentence distribution, boundary stability, and error-free coverage.
Do not let algebraic complements count as independent votes, and do not turn a
raw count into a band.

English and French require separate normalization and cut points. TD and CV
require separate feature groups. Never fit normalization, selection, or
information-gain labels on dev-60 or the protected level-2 cohort.

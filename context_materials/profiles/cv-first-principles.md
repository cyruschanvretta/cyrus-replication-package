# Conventions first principles

Observe the reader's work. Keep intelligibility, repair scope, recurrence, dispersion,
and demonstrated control separate. Error counts alone cannot decide whether meaning
is blocked, repeatedly reconstructed, briefly interrupted, or read without distraction.

Simple controlled sentences are evidence of control. Ambitious syntax is not evidence
of control when boundaries or relationships repeatedly break down. Judge only the
current response, never its topic, item identity, membership, or apparent quality of
ideas.

# Conventions annotation patterns

Rater descriptions separate a copied local form from varied problems distributed
through the response. Track whether a pattern recurs across sentence boundaries and
whether the reader can repair it immediately or must reconstruct relationships.

Grammar, syntax, sentence boundaries, usage, spelling, punctuation, and capitalization
can have different effects. A visible isolated error may distract less than a subtle
recurring structure problem. Fluency of ideas must not create a convention halo.

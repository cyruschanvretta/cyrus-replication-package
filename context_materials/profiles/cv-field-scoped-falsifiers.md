# CV field-scoped falsifiers

For the requested field, look independently for a control witness and a counter-witness. Reader burden is decided from the response alone. For convention fields, LanguageTool can locate candidates, but accept a candidate only after verifying it in context.

## I
Witness: propositions remain available on an ordinary read. Counter-witness: meaning is delayed, rereading recurs, or a central proposition cannot be recovered.

## R
Witness: any repair is local and preserves the sentence plan. Counter-witness: several relationships or whole propositions must be rebuilt.

## G
Witness: grammatical relationships remain stable. Counter-witness: a verified recurring relationship failure makes sentences hard to resolve.

## S
Witness: boundaries and clause relations are clear. Counter-witness: fused, fragmented, or unstable structures obscure relationships.

## U
Witness: forms and references work in context. Counter-witness: verified usage patterns recur or alter intended relationships.

## P
Witness: punctuation supports boundaries and grouping. Counter-witness: verified punctuation patterns repeatedly blur them.

## O
Witness: orthography permits continuous reading. Counter-witness: verified recurring patterns distract or create ambiguity; duplicates remain one pattern.

## A
Witness: varied structures are controlled. Counter-witness: apparent variety is produced mainly by uncontrolled length or fragments.

## C
Witness: control persists across regions and domains. Counter-witness: a verified pattern recurs across the response or several domains become unstable.

# TD field-scoped falsifiers

For the requested field, look independently for a positive witness and a decisive blocker. Do not quote the response in the output. When a blocker explains why an apparent witness is superficial, classify the observable field conservatively.

## T
Witness: the response makes a task-directed contribution. Blocker: it only echoes wording, associates with the topic, or answers a different task.

## P
Witness: a controlling position is recoverable across the response. Blocker: the position is missing, switches, or is only inferred from isolated phrases.

## B
Witness: independently useful support units. Blocker: units are restatements, subdivisions of one reason, or unsupported claims.

## S
Witness: detail makes a support unit more precise. Blocker: detail is ornamental, generic, or disconnected from the support function.

## L
Witness: the writing establishes why support bears on the position. Blocker: it relies on adjacency, repetition, or a connective without an inferential relationship.

## E
Witness: the response explains mechanisms, reasons, consequences, or significance. Blocker: it mainly names, asserts, lists, or illustrates without unpacking.

## O
Witness: parts have a functional relationship and cumulative direction. Blocker: organization is primarily formula, chronology without purpose, or a list of parallel points.

## A
Witness: later ideas deepen, qualify, connect, or synthesize earlier ones. Blocker: later material repeats or merely adds another parallel example.

## F
Witness: focus, development, and closure functions are actually carried out. Blocker: a conventional phrase labels a role without performing it.

## C
Witness: the line of thought remains compatible or deliberately qualified. Blocker: unresolved contradiction, reference drift, or incompatible claims.

# Topic Development — invariant scoring rubric

Score the development of an extended opinion response independently of spelling,
grammar, punctuation, handwriting, or sentence polish.

- 0 — no scoreable linguistic response.
- 1 — related to the task but no opinion; or an opinion with ideas and no supporting
  details; or details unrelated to the opinion; no evidence of content organization.
- 2 — only part supports an opinion, or the opinion is unclear/inconsistent; links
  between ideas, details, and opinion are not always evident; details are too few or
  repetitive; organization is limited.
- 3 — clear opinion, but supporting details are insufficient or vague, or their link
  to the opinion is sometimes unclear; organization exists but lapses distract.
- 4 — clear, consistent opinion with sufficient supporting details, only some of
  which are specific; organization is mechanical and lapses do not distract.
- 5 — clear, consistent opinion with sufficient, specific supporting details and
  logical organization.
- 6 — clear, consistent opinion with sufficient, specific details that are
  thoughtfully chosen; organization is coherent and ideas progress thoughtfully.

The load-bearing boundaries are: sufficiency at 3/4, consistency of specificity and
logical organization at 4/5, and thoughtful selection/progression at 5/6. Do not use
response length, vocabulary prestige, factual truth, or surface correctness as a
substitute for these constructs.

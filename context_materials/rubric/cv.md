# Conventions — invariant scoring rubric

Score grammar, usage, spelling, punctuation, sentence structure, and syntax. Ignore
the quality, precision, relevance, and organization of ideas; those belong to Topic
Development. Treat the response as a first draft.

- 0 — blank, non-linguistic, or otherwise unscoreable response.
- 1 — fewer than 30 student-authored words, or convention errors interfere with
  communication: reading rhythm is constantly interrupted and repeated rereading is
  needed to recover the overall message.
- 2 — errors distract from communication: the message can be understood, but reading
  rhythm is interrupted and the reader is as aware of errors as of the information.
- 3 — errors do not distract: errors exist but do not impede understanding.
- 4 — control is evident: conventions expected by the end of Grade 9 are used
  consistently and correctly; a few errors may exist.

This is an impact-and-control judgement, not an error hunt. Repeated instances of the
same misspelling count as one spelling problem, and no single category automatically
determines the score except the explicit under-30-word rule.
